This file is a partial climate sensitivity table, not a complete LCA.
For each scenario and EU27 country, it scales the published JRC gypsum climate-saving benchmark to the scenario recovered gypsum equivalent and subtracts only the published Pedreno-Rojas electricity inventory multiplied by the dated 2021 JRC country factor.
Thermal energy, diesel, transport, reject treatment, acid or water treatment, allocation, substitution, market acceptance and the remaining life-cycle inventory are not included.
C0-C3 are transparent researcher-defined scenario bundles, not observed EU27 contamination frequencies.
