Local boundary sensitivity, project 23
Each continuous input is changed one at a time by -25%, -10%, -5%, 0%, +5%, +10% and +25%,
then clipped to its registered bounds. The diagnostic margin is the minimum normalized margin
for purity, moisture and organic-proxy gates. Positive values pass the proxy screen; C2 remains
a visible-contaminant hard-failure branch. This is sensitivity, not empirical validation.
