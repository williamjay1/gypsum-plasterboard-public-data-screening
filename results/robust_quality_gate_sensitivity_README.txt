All-corners boundary sensitivity, project 23
The script enumerates all 2^5 lower/upper parameter corners in the scenario assumption register.
The two gates are engineering diagnostics: Eurogypsum 2024 voluntary values and a German technical comparator.
Purity and organic values are transparent mass proxies, not CaSO4 or TOC measurements.
ALL_CORNERS_PASS means every enumerated corner passes; NO_CORNERS_PASS means none passes; BOUNDARY_INDETERMINATE means the
decision changes within the stated bounds. Hazardous and visible-contaminant flags are hard routing gates.
This is a parametric boundary-sensitivity result, not a European batch prevalence estimate or legal acceptance rule.
